What is IKS?

A Legacy of Enlightenment

The Indian Knowledge System (IKS) represents a continuous, evolving tradition of knowledge creation spanning thousands of years. It encompasses a holistic approach to life — integrating the material and the spiritual, the scientific and the philosophical.

Relevance Today: From Ayurveda's holistic wellness to Yoga's global mental health impact, from zero and calculus to sustainable Vastu architecture — IKS continues to offer timeless solutions to modern challenges.

The Four Sacred Vedas

The Vedas are the oldest scriptures of humanity. Click any card to open its full dedicated page with detailed content.

🔥

ऋग्वेद | Rigveda

The Veda of Hymns & Praise

The oldest of the four Vedas, composed around 1500 BCE, containing 10,552 hymns addressed to natural forces and cosmic deities. It is the bedrock of all Vedic knowledge and includes the Gayatri Mantra and the profound Nasadiya Sukta on creation.

Open Full Page ›
🎵

सामवेद | Samaveda

The Veda of Melody & Music

The Veda of Songs — its 1,875 verses are set to precise musical melodies and are the direct ancestor of Indian classical music. Lord Krishna declares it the greatest Veda in the Bhagavad Gita.

Open Full Page ›
🏹

यजुर्वेद | Yajurveda

The Veda of Ritual & Sacred Action

The practical guidebook for Vedic rituals (Yajnas), the Yajurveda exists in two recensions and houses the Isha Upanishad. Its sacred fire rituals carry deep ecological and communal significance.

Open Full Page ›
🌿

अथर्ववेद | Atharvaveda

The Veda of Life & Healing

The most practical of all Vedas — its 6,000 mantras cover medicine, ecology, governance, and social customs. The Prithvi Sukta, its Hymn to the Earth, is the world's earliest declaration of ecological ethics.
